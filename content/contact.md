## You don't call, you don't write...

We're starting to think you don't love us.

In all seriousness, though, we love meeting new people. Just be cautious; if you get us talking about the things we're passionate about, we may never shut up again. If you're looking to try your luck, here's how to find us:

 * We're on [Twitter](https://www.twitter.com "Twitter"). Send us an @-mention&mdash;we're [@secondbit](https://www.twitter.com/secondbit "Second Bit on Twitter"). You can also [follow the team](https://www.twitter.com/secondbit/team "The Second Bit team on Twitter"), if you think the [people behind Second Bit](/team "Second Bit Team") are as cool as the company is (we are).
 * If [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com "Facebook") is more your thing, don't worry&mdash;we've [got a page](https://www.facebook.com/secondbit "Second Bit on Facebook") set up over there, too, though we must confess we don't use it as much as we use Twitter. Like us! We like you.
 * Love [Google+](https://plus.google.com "Google+")? Good, so do we. We've [got a page](http://via.2ndb.it/plus "Second Bit on Google+") set up over there, too, and we occasionally use it to host public Hangouts, solicit feedback, share what we're working on, or just talk with people.
 * If you're not really into that whole &quot;social media&quot; thing or want a more private form of communication, we're kind of addicted to our email. Send an email to [hello@secondbit.org](mailto:hello@secondbit.org "hello@secondbit.org") and say.. err.. hello.
 * For our open source [projects](/projects "Second Bit's projects"), we use [Github](https://www.github.com "Github"). We'd be flattered if you [forked us](https://www.github.com/secondbit "Second Bit on Github").
 * Need something a bit more instant? Everyone on [our team](/team "Second Bit's super-awesome team") has a Google Talk account set up. Just use their Second Bit email address when adding a buddy, and you can chat with us.
 * Finally, if you want to hear a human voice (sometimes it's nice!), you can always give Paddy a call. His number is +1 (716) 771-2486, and he loves talking to people, so don't hesitate to call. If he doesn't pick up, just leave a voicemail. He'll call you back. Pinky promise.
